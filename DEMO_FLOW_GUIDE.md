# TokenIQ Demo Flow Guide

## 🎬 Complete Demo Script (20 minutes)

### Phase 1: Introduction (2 minutes)

**Start Screen: Dashboard**

1. **Welcome & Context**
   - "TokenIQ is a world-class DeFi platform built on Sei Network"
   - "I'll show you how we leverage Sei's capabilities for AI-powered capital optimization"

2. **Point Out Key Features**
   - "Notice the 'Powered by Sei Intelligence Layer' badge"
   - "Portfolio value: $30,000 with $3,200 projected annual yield"
   - "All data is real-time, powered by Sei's high throughput"

---

### Phase 2: Core Demo - AI Capital Optimization (5 minutes)

**Navigate to: Discover Page**

3. **Show AI Capital Optimization Section**
   - "This is our flagship feature - AI-powered capital optimization"
   - Click to expand the section
   - Point out: "Spending vs. Savings chart with AI analysis"
   - Highlight: "Idle Capital Detected: $45,000"

4. **Demo the Activation Flow** ⭐ **CRITICAL MOMENT**
   - Click "Activate TokenIQ Intelligence"
   - **Say**: "This triggers a real blockchain transaction"
   - Wallet popup appears
   - **Say**: "You can see transaction details - Sei Network, gas fee ~0.001 SEI"
   - Click "Confirm Transaction"
   - **Say**: "Notice how fast Sei confirms - this is Sei's <100ms finality"
   - Show progress: "Allocating funds to Sei yield pools..."
   - **Say**: "Watch the AI allocate funds in real-time"
   - Watch animation: Sei (40%), USDC (33.3%), Bitcoin (26.7%)
   - **Say**: "This is all happening on-chain, on Sei Network"
   - Success: "Done!" message appears

**Key Talking Points**:
- "Sei's parallel execution enables real-time AI allocation"
- "Low fees make micro-optimizations profitable"
- "This is the power of Sei Intelligence Layer"

---

### Phase 3: Portfolio Management (5 minutes)

**Navigate to: Portfolio Page**

5. **Show Strategy Cards**
   - Point out three strategies:
     - Conservative: $22,000 (8.5% APY, Low Risk)
     - Balanced: $4,000 (10.2% APY, Medium Risk)
     - Growth: $4,000 (12.8% APY, High Risk)
   - **Say**: "Each strategy has a yield projection chart"
   - **Say**: "These are powered by Sei Network data"

6. **Demo Emergency Withdraw** ⭐ **IMPORTANT MOMENT**
   - Click "Emergency Withdraw" on Conservative strategy
   - **Say**: "This demonstrates Sei's fast finality for critical operations"
   - Wallet popup → Confirm
   - **Say**: "Transaction confirmed on Sei Network"
   - Show state change:
     - Funds Allocated → "Funds Restored" (green)
     - Yield chart → "0% - Funds Recalled"
     - Button → "Funds Restored" (disabled)
   - **Say**: "Instant state updates thanks to Sei's speed"

7. **Show Token Holdings**
   - Point out: SEI, USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH
   - **Say**: "All tokens on Sei Network with current market prices"
   - Show recent transactions tab

**Key Talking Points**:
- "Sei Network enables instant portfolio updates"
- "Emergency withdrawals are critical - Sei delivers speed"
- "All strategies optimized for Sei's low fees"

### Phase 4: AI Insights (4 minutes)

10. **Show Recommendations**
